# Privacy Policy — Puzzle Vault

_Last updated: 2026-09-11_

Puzzle Vault ("the app") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any
personal data, and does not require an internet connection to function.

## What the app stores

The app saves your game progress (which levels you've unlocked, star
ratings, best scores) and your preferences (dark/light theme, sound,
animations) **only on your device**, using the operating system's standard
local app-storage mechanism. This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is never transmitted to us or to any third party,
- is never used for advertising, analytics, or tracking,
- is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app, or manually via the
  in-app Settings > Reset progress options.

## Third-party services

The app uses no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising networks, or
crash-reporting services. It makes no network requests of any kind.

## Permissions

The app requests no special device permissions (no camera, location,
contacts, microphone, network, or storage access beyond its own private
app-data folder, which the OS grants automatically).

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from
children either. The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat, and
no user-generated or user-submitted content.

## Changes to this policy

If this app ever changes to collect data, transmit data, or add
third-party services, this policy will be updated first and the update
will be reflected in both store listings.
